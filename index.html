WHAT IS UNDECKED?

UNDECKED is a chaotic card game for 2–6 players.


Everyone starts with 7 cards. Players can steal cards, give cards, force opponents to draw and destroy their hands.


Your cards can disappear quickly. Reach 0 cards and you're out. The last player with cards wins.

THE CARDS
